TITLE: Election Myths, Reality & Verified Facts (India)

SOURCE: Election Commission of India (ECI), PIB Fact Check

---

SECTION: Voting Eligibility

MYTH:
A person not in voter list can vote using ID proof.

REALITY:
Only individuals listed in the electoral roll can vote. Identity is verified against the roll at polling station.

---

MYTH:
If name is missing, you can use "Challenge Vote" to vote.

REALITY:
Challenge vote is only for identity disputes of registered voters. It cannot be used by unregistered individuals.

---

SECTION: Home Voting & Postal Ballots

MYTH:
Political party agents cannot accompany polling teams during home voting.

REALITY:
Authorized representatives of recognized political parties can be present with proper authorization (Form 10).

---

MYTH:
Secrecy of vote is compromised in home voting.

REALITY:
Strict protocols including supervision and verification ensure secrecy of voting.

---

MYTH:
Postal ballots are unreliable.

REALITY:
Postal ballots are officially regulated and used by service voters, PwDs, and senior citizens (80+).

---

SECTION: Polling Station Facilities

MYTH:
Polling stations lack basic facilities.

REALITY:
All polling stations provide Assured Minimum Facilities (AMF):
- Drinking water
- Waiting area
- Toilets
- Lighting
- Ramp access
- Voting compartments :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

MYTH:
Polling stations are inaccessible.

REALITY:
Polling stations are designed with ramps and ground-floor access for accessibility.

---

SECTION: Digital Services

MYTH:
Saksham ECI app is not useful.

REALITY:
It provides:
- Registration
- Correction
- Migration
- Aadhaar authentication
- Polling station details
- Complaint services

---

SECTION: Identity & Documents

MYTH:
Disability card (UDID) cannot be used.

REALITY:
UDID is a valid identification document for voting.

---

SECTION: Election Rules Misconceptions

MYTH:
Repoll happens if tender votes exceed 14%.

REALITY:
No such rule exists. Repoll is governed by legal provisions under election law.

---

MYTH:
Money is deducted if a person does not vote.

REALITY:
Voting is not compulsory in India. No penalties exist. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

SECTION: EVM & Voting System

MYTH:
EVMs can be hacked.

REALITY:
EVMs are standalone machines not connected to networks. Claims of hacking are misleading.

---

MYTH:
Voters can vote multiple times.

REALITY:
Indelible ink and voter verification prevent multiple voting. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

SECTION: Fake News & Disinformation Cases

CASE:
Fake election schedule circulated on WhatsApp.

FACT:
Election schedules are announced officially via ECI press conferences. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

CASE:
Video showing election rigging shared online.

FACT:
The video was unrelated or from old elections and falsely linked to current elections. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

---

CASE:
Claim of duplicate voting using artificial fingers.

FACT:
The image was unrelated and falsely used. No such practice exists. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

---

CASE:
Claim of 100%+ voter turnout.

FACT:
Higher turnout occurred due to inclusion of election duty voters (EDC), not fraud. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

---

CASE:
Claim that indelible ink is made from animal fat.

FACT:
Indelible ink is made using silver nitrate and is scientifically developed. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

---

SECTION: Election Authority Clarification

FACT:
Election Commission of India is responsible for conducting national and state elections.

FACT:
Local body elections are conducted by State Election Commissions, not ECI. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}
