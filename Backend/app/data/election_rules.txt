TITLE: Election Rules and Candidate Guidelines (India)

SOURCE: Election Commission of India (ECI)

---

SECTION: Eligibility to Contest Elections

RULE:
Minimum age to contest Lok Sabha or Vidhan Sabha elections is 25 years. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

RULE:
A person must be registered as a voter in an electoral roll to contest elections. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

RULE:
A person registered as a voter in one state can contest Lok Sabha elections from another state (with exceptions like certain regions). :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

RULE:
A Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe candidate can contest from reserved or general constituencies. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

RULE:
A person convicted and sentenced to imprisonment (2 years or more) is disqualified from contesting elections. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

---

SECTION: Voting Eligibility

RULE:
A person in prison cannot vote in elections. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

RULE:
A person under preventive detention is allowed to vote via postal ballot. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

---

SECTION: Security Deposit

RULE:
Security deposit for Lok Sabha election:
- General: ₹10,000  
- SC/ST: ₹5,000 :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

RULE:
Security deposit for Vidhan Sabha election:
- General: ₹5,000  
- SC/ST: ₹2,500 :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

RULE:
Deposit is forfeited if candidate fails to secure at least one-sixth of valid votes. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

---

SECTION: Nomination Rules

RULE:
Recognized party candidate requires only 1 proposer. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

RULE:
Independent or unrecognized party candidate requires 10 proposers. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

RULE:
A candidate can contest from a maximum of 2 constituencies. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

RULE:
Maximum 4 nomination papers can be filed in one constituency. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}

RULE:
Only limited number of people are allowed during nomination filing and scrutiny. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}

---

SECTION: Oath Requirement

RULE:
Candidate must take oath or affirmation before an authorized officer. :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15}

RULE:
Oath must be completed before scrutiny of nominations. :contentReference[oaicite:16]{index=16}

---

SECTION: Election Symbols

RULE:
Symbols are allotted by the Returning Officer. :contentReference[oaicite:17]{index=17}

RULE:
Recognized party candidates must submit Form A and Form B for symbol allocation. :contentReference[oaicite:18]{index=18}

RULE:
Independent candidates can choose from available free symbols. :contentReference[oaicite:19]{index=19}

---

SECTION: Campaign Rules

RULE:
Vehicles used for campaigning require prior approval from Returning Officer. :contentReference[oaicite:20]{index=20}

RULE:
Campaign meetings and processions are prohibited 48 hours before polling ends. :contentReference[oaicite:21]{index=21}

RULE:
Campaign offices cannot be set up near polling stations or in restricted areas (schools, hospitals, religious places). :contentReference[oaicite:22]{index=22}

RULE:
Distribution of items like clothes to voters is considered bribery and is not allowed. :contentReference[oaicite:23]{index=23}

---

SECTION: Poll Day Rules

RULE:
Voter assistance booths are provided to help locate names in electoral roll. :contentReference[oaicite:24]{index=24}

RULE:
Election booths must be set up at least 200 meters away from polling stations. :contentReference[oaicite:25]{index=25}

RULE:
Canvassing is prohibited within 100 meters of polling station. :contentReference[oaicite:26]{index=26}

RULE:
Carrying weapons near polling station is prohibited. :contentReference[oaicite:27]{index=27}

---

SECTION: Voting Methods

RULE:
Postal ballot is available for:
- Service voters
- Election duty staff
- Preventive detention voters :contentReference[oaicite:28]{index=28}

RULE:
Proxy voting is allowed only for certain service voters. :contentReference[oaicite:29]{index=29}

---

SECTION: Election Monitoring

RULE:
Micro observers are deployed to ensure fair polling and monitor critical activities. :contentReference[oaicite:30]{index=30}

---

SECTION: Election Expenditure

RULE:
Candidates must not exceed prescribed expenditure limits. :contentReference[oaicite:31]{index=31}

RULE:
Candidates must submit election expense reports within 30 days of result. :contentReference[oaicite:32]{index=32}

RULE:
Failure to submit expense report can lead to disqualification for up to 3 years. :contentReference[oaicite:33]{index=33}
